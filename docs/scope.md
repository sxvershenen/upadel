# MVP scope

## Complete

- Astro/Payload/PostgreSQL foundation, immutable prototype baseline and CMS-backed homepage.
- Home/site settings, single-source business collections, idempotent demo seed and live preview.
- Media WebP derivatives, visual Gallery admin, reverse usage locations and deletion guard.
- Blog, coach and tournament catalog/detail routes with filters and SEO fields.
- Prices, training, courts, gallery and about routes with accepted responsive layouts; `/contacts` is a compatibility redirect to `/about`.
- Policy and oferta routes with explicit `noindex` legal placeholders pending approved copy.
- Contact confirmation dialogs, membership/gift/trial forms and authenticated Leads workflow.
- Consent-aware first-party analytics, four-tab dashboard, ordered funnels, mergeable uniques and safe manual/automatic retention.
- Code-defined page map, managed internal redirects, self-canonicals and published/indexable sitemap/robots output.
- External analytics settings with consent-gated loaders, masked notification credentials, delivery status/retry and safe booking fallback.
- Media size/MIME enforcement, image derivatives and usable image/video previews with usage locations.

## Next

- Production configuration, approved content/assets and provider credentials.

## Remaining CMS

- Booking provider implementation after VivaCRM/Lunda/Padel app details are known.
- Media usage registry updates for future relationship fields; production video transcoding/object storage after hosting is selected.

## Deferred or externally blocked

- Confirmed booking/payment and call completion require provider/call-tracking integrations.
- Automated tournament/booking sync requires a selected provider API.
- Legal document copy and final production contacts/assets require owner/legal approval.
